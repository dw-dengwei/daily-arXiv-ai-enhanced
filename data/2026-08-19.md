Motivation: 聚类图（团的不相交并）是一类结构简单的图，其同构类与整数分拆一一对应。理解这类图上编辑距离的度量几何和计算复杂性，需要找到连接两者的统一数学身份。本文的动机正是建立这样一个身份，从而同时给出度量性质（ℓ₁模型、常数界、欧几里得扭曲度）和计算复杂性（NP完全性、近似困难性）的系统刻画。

Method: 本文的核心方法是通过一个关键身份将商编辑距离q*转化为列联表上Frobenius范数平方最大值的仿射函数。由此：(1) 推导出两个显式ℓ₁度量模型（顶点质量度量δ₁和块能量度量B），并给出最优常数界；(2) 通过加权Ferrers阶梯的dyadic和F^(γ)分析欧几里得扭曲度，利用阶梯跳跃的量化证明反能量不等式；(3) 利用同一身份进行分类，证明判定问题的强NP完全性和评估问题的强NP困难性。

Result: 主要结果包括：度量界½δ₁≤q*<3/2δ₁（两常数最优）和q*≤B≤2q*-1；c₁(𝒦ₙ)≤2；O(n log n)时间算法返回成本低于2q*的比对及q*∈[⌈(B+1)/2⌉,B]的证书；欧几里得扭曲度c₂(𝒦ₙ)=Θ(n^{1/4})；未加权成员扭曲度为Θ(n^{1/4}√(log n))，临界权重γ=¼无条件改进到O(n^{1/4}(log n)^{1/4})；判定q*(λ,μ)≤Q为强NP完全，评估为强NP困难且无FPTAS。

Conclusion: 本文通过单一身份同时揭示了聚类图编辑距离的度量几何与计算复杂性，建立了度量性质（ℓ₁嵌入、欧几里得扭曲、最优常数）与计算难度（NP完全性、不可近似性）之间的深刻联系。剩余的(log n)^{1/4}因子的消除被归约为实锥上的一个反不等式问题，为后续研究指明了方向。

Abstract: The cluster graphs on $n$ vertices, the disjoint unions of complete graphs, have the integer partitions of $n$ as their isomorphism classes, and the quotient edit distance $q^*(λ,μ)=\min_{σ\in S_n}|E(G_λ)\triangleσE(G_μ)|$ makes that set a metric space. Its metric geometry and its computational complexity both issue from one identity: $q^*$ is an affine function of the maximum of $\lVert X\rVert_F^2$ over the contingency tables with margins $λ$ and $μ$. Combinatorially, it yields two explicit $\ell_1$ models: the vertex-mass metric $δ_1$ on sorted degree sequences, with $\frac12δ_1\le q^*<\frac32δ_1$ and both constants optimal, and the block-energy metric $B$ on the vectors $\bigl(\binom{λ_i}2\bigr)_i$, with $q^*\le B\le2q^*-1$ by a per-table refinement measuring how far an alignment is from a block bijection. Hence $c_1(\mathcal K_n)\le2$, and an $O(n\log n)$-time algorithm returns an alignment of cost below $2q^*$ with the certificate $q^*\in[\lceil(B+1)/2\rceil,B]$. The Euclidean distortion of the class is $c_2(\mathcal K_n)=Θ(n^{1/4})$; against it we measure the weighted dyadic sums $F^{(γ)}$ of the Ferrers staircase, of dimension below $4n$ and computable in $O(n)$ time. The unweighted member has distortion exactly $Θ(n^{1/4}\sqrt{\log n})$, while the critical weight $γ=\frac14$ improves this unconditionally to $O(n^{1/4}(\log n)^{1/4})$ through an inverse energy inequality proved from the quantization of staircase jumps; removing the residual $(\log n)^{1/4}$ is reduced to one inverse inequality on the realizable cone. Computationally, the same identity gives a classification: deciding $q^*(λ,μ)\le Q$ is strongly NP-complete, evaluation is strongly NP-hard and admits no FPTAS unless $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$, while the farthest alignment is polynomial-time solvable.
